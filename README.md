The world in one day — Tuesday 24 November 2026, summed up in a single 1024×1024 image.

The front page leads with four tankers hitting mines in the Strait of Hormuz, then covers the day's other big stories: Huawei found guilty by a US jury, the Hamas security chief killed in Gaza, the overnight attack on Zaporizhzhia, Jensen Huang's return as Nvidia CEO, Sanofi's $41 billion deal for Insmed, and the Nepal earthquake — plus the ICJ Rohingya ruling, Senegal's Africa Cup of Nations title and the UN's call for AI rules.

The image is drawn programmatically from `render.html` (headless Chromium screenshot, so every word is crisp), and `main.ts` copies the finished `entry.png` to the output folder.
